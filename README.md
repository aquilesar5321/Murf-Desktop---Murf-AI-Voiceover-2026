## 💡 Pro Tips for Amazing Voiceovers

- **Punctuation Matters** – Use commas, periods, question marks, and exclamations to guide the AI's pacingand intonation. Long pauses? Use ellipses (...).
- **Emphasize Words** – Write key words in **ALL CAPS** to make the AI stress them naturally( but avoid shouting too much text}}.
- **Match Pitch to Mood** – Lower pitch (-2 to -4) for authoritative, serious narrations. Higher pitch (+2 to +4) for energetic, playful ads.

- **Layer Music Wisely** – Keep background music volume at 10-20% of the voice track to ensure clarity.
